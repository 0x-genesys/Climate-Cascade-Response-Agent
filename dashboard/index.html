    Reference

    Glossary: terms used in this review

    Each term names a source, control, or checkpoint in the response flow.

    CEMS

    Copernicus Emergency Management Service. The primary public mapping source for this flood MVP. Used first during source intake to identify the activation, affected-area products, and reported impacts.

    USGS

    U.S. Geological Survey. A technical hazard source. Used during source intake to preserve preliminary trigger findings and prevent unsupported certainty.

    International Charter

    Space and Major Disasters. A disaster-response satellite-imagery coordination source. Used during source intake to corroborate activation context and data gaps.

    AOI

    Area of interest. A named mapping area within a CEMS activation. Used in Official Mapping to show whether a product is delivered, pending, or unavailable.

    Evidence snapshot

    A pinned copy of a source response. It stores a URL, retrieval time, checksum, and immutable ID. Used to ground every draft action and evidence-reference check.

    Response supervisor

    The bounded drafting agent. After evidence is saved, it produces at most five human-reviewable actions. It cannot dispatch, warn, or approve anything.

    Impact analysis

    Deterministic product-statistics extraction. Before drafting, the worker reads saved CEMS product facts such as affected population, residential buildings, facilities, roads, and bridges. It preserves missing coverage instead of guessing.

    Draft only

    A proposal, not an instruction. It appears after the response supervisor succeeds. A qualified emergency manager still owns any approval, edit, evidence request, or rejection.

    Run Feedback

    Automatic pre-review checks. It verifies autonomous-action safety and evidence references after drafting. Passing it means the queue is ready for human review, not that the actions are approved.

    LSAC@5

    Life-Safety Action Coverage at 5. The weighted share of frozen life-safety requirements covered by the top five drafts. Used only after a human records coverage decisions for the Nepal practice case.

    Human adjudication

    The explicit coverage review. A reviewer decides whether each frozen requirement is actually covered, then the deterministic evaluator calculates LSAC@5 for comparison with the baseline.

    Not estimable

    A required abstention when evidence is insufficient. It means the system will not invent a lives-saved range. Used beside an action when Iteration 1 lacks calibrated impact support.

    SSE progress feed

    Server-Sent Events. The live, ordered workflow stream used by Run Progress and the current status message. It reports observable work, not private model reasoning.
