Climate Cascade Response

Turn a verified disaster map into a ranked queue of owned, time-bound, human-reviewed actions with population impact, evidence, uncertainty, and modelled life-safety benefit.

The product supports decisions after a disaster is verified. It never predicts collapse, orders evacuation, dispatches responders, or claims that exposed population equals lives saved.

Architecture

Visible, resumable agent lifecycle

flowchart TD A["Receive activation"] --> B["Verify authoritative sources"] B -->|conflict or missing| X["Block and explain"] B -->|verified| C["Snapshot data and licenses"] C --> D["Deterministic impact analysis"] D --> E["Draft typed actions"] E --> F["Verify evidence and estimates"] F -->|failed| E F --> G["Human review"] G -->|request evidence or edit| E G -->|approve| H["Export action brief and audit"]

Core product

Action, not another map

  • Where: linked map geometry and affected population
  • What: one concrete verb-first response step
  • Who: accountable human owner
  • Why: cited evidence and calculation
  • Impact: modelled range or not estimable
